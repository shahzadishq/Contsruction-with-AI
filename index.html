How a building goes up

Scroll to watch one of our towers rise. Every project follows the same five stages, and you get a written report at the end of each one.

  1. 1

    Survey and soil test

    We test the ground before we price the job, so the foundation is designed for your plot and not a guess.

  2. 2

    Foundation

    Piles and raft poured to the engineer's drawing, with cube tests from every pour.

  3. 3

    Structure

    Columns and slabs go up a floor at a time. You can visit the site any working day.

  4. 4

    Finishing

    Facade, plumbing, wiring and fittings, checked against the snag list you sign off.

  5. 5

    Handover

    Keys, drawings, warranties and a one-year defects promise.
